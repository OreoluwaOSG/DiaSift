Rapid-acting insulin Brands: Admelog, Apidra, Fiasp, Humalog, Lyumjev, NovoRapid, Trurapi. Includes insulin aspart, lispro and glulisine.
Short-acting insulin Brands: Actrapid, Humulin S. Also known as soluble insulin.
Intermediate-acting insulin Brands: Humulin I, Insulatard. Also known as isophane insulin.
Long-acting insulin Brands: Abasaglar, Lantus, Levemir, Semglee, Toujeo, Tresiba. Includes insulin detemir, glargine and degludec.
Biphasic insulin Brands: Humalog Mix, Humulin M3, NovoMix. Also known as mixed insulin. Includes biphasic aspart, lispro and isophane.
